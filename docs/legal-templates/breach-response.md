# Security breach response plan — [Organisation name]

> Draft. POPIA s22: where there are reasonable grounds to believe personal
> information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, the
> organisation must notify the Information Regulator and the affected people
> as soon as reasonably possible.

## Anyone who suspects a breach

Tell the Information Officer **immediately** — [name, phone, email]. Examples:
a lost phone or laptop with portal access, a leader's password shared or
stolen, member information sent to the wrong person, a printed list left
behind, something odd in the portal.

## Information Officer — first 24 hours

1. **Contain:** reset the affected passwords, remove the person's access in
   Settings → Team & access, revoke sessions in the Supabase dashboard,
   rotate any exposed keys (Supabase, Mux, Resend), and recall misdirected
   emails where possible.
2. **Record:** what happened, when it was noticed, what information and how
   many people may be affected (the portal's audit log helps).
3. **Assess:** could it cause harm (identity theft, financial loss,
   embarrassment, exposure of religious affiliation or giving)?

## Notify (as soon as reasonably possible)

- **Information Regulator**, using its prescribed security-compromise
  notification process.
- **Affected people**, in writing (email to their last known address, or
  another reliable way), including: what happened; what information was
  involved; what we've done; what they should do (e.g. watch for phishing,
  change passwords); and the Information Officer's contact details.
- Delay only if a public body involved in investigating a crime asks for it.
