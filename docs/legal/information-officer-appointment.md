# Designation of Information Officer — The Haven Zone E4

> Draft. Under POPIA and PAIA the head of a private body is its Information
> Officer unless they designate (delegate) someone in writing. Use this if the
> head wants someone else to carry the day-to-day duties. Register the
> Information Officer (and deputies) with the Information Regulator.

I, **[name of head]**, as **[title]** and head of **The Haven Zone E4**,
designate:

- **[Name]** as Information Officer, and
- **[Name]** as Deputy Information Officer [delete if none],

with effect from **[date]**.

Their duties include:

- encouraging and monitoring compliance with POPIA and PAIA;
- handling requests from people about their personal information (in the
  portal: Settings → Privacy requests) within the legal time limits;
- working with the Information Regulator, including on any investigation;
- keeping the PAIA manual, privacy notice, operator register and retention
  schedule current;
- leading the response to any security breach;
- making sure leaders understand their confidentiality obligations.

This designation does not remove the head's own responsibility.
