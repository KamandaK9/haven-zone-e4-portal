## 6. Processing of personal information (POPIA)

- **Purpose:** running the organisation's membership, pastoral care,
  leadership, finances, training, events and communications; meeting legal
  obligations.
- **Categories of data subjects and information:** members (identity, contact,
  membership, religious affiliation, contributions, training); leaders (as
  members, plus position and access); suppliers and correspondents (contact
  details, correspondence).
- **Recipients:** authorised leaders according to role; the service providers
  listed in the operator register; [regional/national bodies, if any];
  authorities where the law requires.
- **Transborder flows:** some service providers store or process information
  outside South Africa (see the operator register); we use providers bound by
  agreements or laws giving comparable protection.
- **Security measures:** role-based access enforced by the database, private
  document storage with short-lived links, encryption in transit, optional
  two-factor sign-in, automatic sign-out, audit logging, regular review.

## 7. How to request access

1. Complete the prescribed request form (Form 2, available from the
   Information Regulator) and send it to the Information Officer at the
   contact details above. Members may also use “Your data” in the portal.
2. Give enough detail to identify the record and the form of access wanted,
   and proof of identity. A requester acting for someone else must show
   authority.
3. We will respond within 30 days (extendable once by up to 30 days with
   notice and reasons).
4. Prescribed fees may apply to requests for records other than your own
   personal information. You will be told of any fee before work begins.

## 8. Grounds for refusal

Access may be refused on the grounds set out in PAIA, for example to protect
another person's privacy, confidential commercial information, or legally
privileged records. Reasons will be given.

## 9. Remedies

If dissatisfied with our decision, you may complain to the Information
Regulator or apply to court as PAIA provides.
